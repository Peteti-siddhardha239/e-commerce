# 🪐 SidduKart — 3D Hyper-Commerce & Next-Gen Spatial Shopping Platform

Inspired by **Flipkart** and **Amazon**, **SidduKart** is a cutting-edge e-commerce web application featuring **3D Product Visualizers**, **Live 3D Shipping Route Tracking**, **Detailed Cost Breakdowns**, **Booking & Checkout Flows**, and **SuperCoins Loyalty Rewards**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🧊 Interactive 3D Product Visualizer (Three.js)
- **360° Free Rotation & Zoom**: Inspect flagship gadgets (iPhone 16 Pro Max, Galaxy S25 Ultra, MacBook Pro M3, iPad Pro M4, Sony A7 IV Camera, AirPods Pro 2, Marshall Speaker, Nintendo Switch, Bose Ultra, PS5 Pro, Jordans, Drones, Meta Ray-Bans) in photorealistic 3D.
- **Custom 3D Procedural Generators**: Dedicated geometric models for **Smartphones**, **Laptops**, **Tablets with Docked Stylus**, **Mirrorless Cameras with Zoom Lens & EVF**, **Wireless Earbuds in Charging Case**, **Vintage Acoustic Speakers with Brass Dials**, **Smartwatches**, **Sneakers**, **Consoles**, and **Quadcopters**.
- **Real-Time Color & Finish Customizer**: Change titanium, metallic, or leather finishes with dynamic PBR shaders.
- **💥 Exploded View Mode**: Smoothly expands internal smartphone layers, camera lenses, laptop lids, tablet styluses, and drone arms outward in 3D.
- **Studio Lighting Presets**: Switch between *Clean Studio Neutral*, *Cyberpunk Neon*, and *Warm Sunset*.

### 2. 🚚 3D Live Shipping Path & Delivery Route Simulator
- **Interactive 3D Route Map**: Watch your package travel from the **Bengaluru Automated Warehouse** → **Express Air Cargo Transit** → **Hyderabad Central Sorting Hub** → **Madhapur Station** → **Your Doorstep**.
- **Moving 3D Vehicles**: Low-poly 3D delivery truck with spinning wheels and aerial delivery drone navigating along the spline.
- **Live Courier Telemetry**:
  - Live Courier Agent Card (*Raju V.* with 4.9 rating and direct contact).
  - Secure **Delivery OTP** verification.
  - Timestamped checkpoint history with live progress indicators.

### 3. 💳 Booking, Cart & Accurate Cost Breakdown
- **Flipkart & Amazon Style Price Breakdown**:
  - Total MRP & Maximum Retail Price savings
  - Discount on MRP (highlighted in emerald green)
  - Delivery Charges (*FREE for Siddu Prime & Assured*)
  - Secured Packaging & Platform handling fees
  - Promotional Coupon Codes:
    - `SIDDU50` — Flat ₹500 off on orders above ₹1,999
    - `FESTIVAL15` — 15% Instant Discount (up to ₹3,000)
    - `FIRSTBUY` — ₹250 New Shopper Voucher
  - **🪙 SuperCoins Redemption**: Deduct SuperCoins for instant rupee discounts.
- **Express Multi-Step Checkout**:
  1. Address selection & GPS auto-detection
  2. Delivery speed (*Prime 1-Day*, *2-Hour Hyperlocal Drone*, *Standard*)
  3. Payment Gateway (*SidduPay UPI QR*, *3D Credit Card with chip*, *Net Banking*, *Cash on Delivery*)
  4. Golden Confetti particle explosion & audio fanfare upon order placement!

### 4. 📄 Printable GST Tax Invoice
- Official, GSTIN-compliant tax invoice generation with detailed tax breakups (18% IGST), HSN codes, and print-to-PDF support.

### 5. ⚖️ Compare Products
- Side-by-side comparison of up to 3 products comparing specs, prices, ratings, and features.

### 6. 📐 Screen-Fit & Auto-Scale (Zero Viewport Clipping)
- Built-in responsive screen-fit engine that automatically fits 1366×768 laptops, 1080p, and high-DPI displays without horizontal or vertical clipping.
- Press `F` to cycle UI scale (`Auto` → `85%` → `92%` → `100%`).
